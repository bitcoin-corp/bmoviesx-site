How it works

Your film, your token.

Four steps, and you are in control of every one.

  1. 1

    Upload your film

    Add a film, a scene, a trailer or a short clip. Already featured from X? Claim it by signing in with your X account.

    Next
  2. 2

    Mint from your bWalletX

    You mint it yourself, from your own wallet. You pay the small network fee and nobody else touches the key.

    Next
  3. 3

    You keep the token and the credit

    The token sits in your wallet with your name on it. Fans can collect and trade films you have minted.

    Next
  4. 4

    It shows in the feed

    Your film appears in the bWalletX feed and across the bApps, including bMovies.app.

    Next

We never mint on your behalf. Nothing goes on chain in your name unless you mint it from your own wallet. Films you have not minted cannot be traded.
