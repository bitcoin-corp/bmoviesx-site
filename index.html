How it works

Your film, your token.

Four steps, and you're in control of every one.

  1. Planned

    Upload your film or clip

    Add a film, a scene, a trailer or a short clip. If we've already featured your work from X, you can claim it by signing in with your X account.

  2. Planned

    Mint it yourself

    You mint from your own bWalletX. You pay the small network fee and you own the token.

  3. Planned

    It shows up everywhere

    Your film appears in the bWalletX feed and across the bApps, with your name on it.

  4. Planned

    Fans can collect it

    Once you've minted it, fans can collect and trade your film's tokens. Films you haven't minted can't be traded.

We never mint on your behalf. Nothing goes on chain in your name unless you mint it from your own wallet.
